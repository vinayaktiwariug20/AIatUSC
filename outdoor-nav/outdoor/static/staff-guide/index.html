Staff guide

For USC Transportation staff and the Doorstep team. Everything here is on the map page: scroll down to Staff. What you save changes routes for everyone within seconds.

You can only restrict, never open. A closure, a "Full" or a "Closed" never sends anyone through a gate the published hours keep shut. Prices you post only change what drivers are told.

1. Sign in with your staff key

  1. Type your key (a few words) into the box under Staff. It says who you are when it's accepted.
  2. Tick Remember on this phone only on your own phone, never on a shared computer.

Keys come from the Doorstep team, one per person or desk. Don't share one in a group chat. If a key is lost, ask the team to revoke it and make a new one; nobody else is affected. Ten different wrong keys within 15 minutes lock staff actions from that connection for up to 30 minutes.

2. Close a gate or an area

  1. Choose Gate closure and the gate, or Area closure, then Draw area and tap the corners of the area on the map.
  2. Choose who it's closed to: cars, people on foot, or both.
  3. Tap how long: 1 hr, 2 hr, 4 hr, Until 8 pm, Rest of today or Overnight, until 6 am. The line underneath says exactly what will be saved. For anything else, open Exact times.
  4. Add a short reason (people see it), then Save confirmed closure.

It appears under Active and upcoming, where Lift ends it early. It ends by itself at its end time.

3. Parking structure status

Swapped the sign at a booth? Open Booth sign, tap the card that's up now (for example EVENT PARKING $40 or LOT FULL), check the line underneath, then Save. When the general sign goes back up, tap Parking available: it lifts the event price, closure or Full you posted, and the usual prices show again. Only structures whose cards the Doorstep team has listed appear there (Royal for now); if a card is missing, tell the team.

For anything else, open Parking structure status, pick the structure and who it applies to.

  • Full or Filling (game days, events): Doorstep stops sending drivers to a full structure and prefers others when one is filling. Add the spaces left if you know them. A live status is shown as possibly out of date after 2 hours, so post again when it changes.
  • Closed to them, with Repeats on these days and times: for this week's Text2Park closures, e.g. Downey, Mon–Thu 6 am–4 pm, for daily parkers only.

4. Parking prices

Doorstep already shows USC Transportation's published rates for each structure. Post only what's different, under Parking rates:

  • Event: an event price (e.g. $45 for a Shrine concert). It replaces the other prices while it applies, then they come back by themselves. If people not at the event can pay less with a validation code, enter that price and how to get a code (e.g. "ask the attendant at the booth"). Never post the code itself: anyone can read this.
  • Free: replaces every other price while it applies.
  • Hourly or All day: a changed rate; it replaces only that one.

5. Reports from the public

People can report a blocked path. Reports show as unverified until you approve one (routes then avoid it) or reject it.

Good to know

  • Times are Los Angeles time.
  • Questions people ask Doorstep's assistant that it can't answer go to the Doorstep team, not to you.
  • A staff key can't see usage numbers, those questions, or other people's keys.
  • Something wrong on the map, a gate that is never open, a new price? Tell the Doorstep team; that's how the map stays right.
